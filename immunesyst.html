The immune system is like a body's own personal defense team that works hard to fight off germs and keep you from getting sick. It uses special organs like your spleen and bone marrow to create white blood cells that act as tiny soldiers against bacteria and viruses. To keep it strong, you should eat healthy food, exercise, and get plenty of sleep so your body is always ready to protect you.

Stay hydrated to keep your protective mucus moist and avoid smoking to protect the tiny hairs that sweep germs out of your lungs.

The Role of Rest

Quality sleep is essential for protein repair and overall immune function. Establishing a consistent routine and avoiding alcohol or screens before bed improves rest quality.

Establish relaxation techniques like mindfulness or yoga to mitigate chronic stress, which can otherwise suppress immune function.

04. Medical Interventions

MED_13000

Vaccines & Memory

Vaccines train the immune system to recognize and defend against specific viruses and bacteria by introducing a harmless version of a germ.

Antibiotics

Antibiotics are medications designed to treat active bacterial infections by killing the bacteria or preventing multiplication. They are ineffective against viral illnesses like the flu.

The immune system stops working correctly when it is too weak or gets confused and starts attacking healthy parts of the body by mistake.
