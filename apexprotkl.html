1. Setup & Connectivity

2.4 GHz Wireless Connection

01 Connect the USB-C Wireless Dongle to your device.
02 Toggle the Wireless Mode Switch to the 2.4 GHz position.
03 The keyboard will pair automatically. Download SteelSeries GG for advanced tuning.

2. Bluetooth Pairing & Switching

The Apex Pro TKL can store up to 3 device profiles.

To Pair: Press SteelSeries Key + Enter + (1, 2, or 3) for 3 seconds.
To Switch: Press SteelSeries Key + Enter + (1, 2, or 3) briefly.

3. OLED & Multimedia Controls

ControlAction
Volume RollerScroll Up/Down for Volume; Press to Mute.
OLED Menu ButtonSingle Press: Play/Pause | Double: Skip | Triple: Back.
Menu AccessHold OLED Menu Button for 2 seconds to enter settings.

4. Actuation Adjustment

Fine-tune your OmniPoint 2.0 switches on the fly:

  • Lighter Touch: SteelSeries Key + O (Decreases actuation point).
  • Deeper Press: SteelSeries Key + I (Increases actuation point).
  • Rapid Trigger: SteelSeries Key + T to toggle.

5. Battery Indicator

Low Battery: Keyboard pulses RED when below 5%.
Charging: The ESC key flashes green; number row indicates current charge level.
